Who can act

The higher the level, the more orders one decision reaches.

This is why the strongest call is directed at lawmakers.

CongressOne law covers every platform and online store.
DENR / NSWMCOne rule covers every company under EPR.
PlatformOne packing rule or checkout default changes millions of orders.
Barangay / LGUOne take-back point serves a community.
SellerOne packing decision changes one shop.
ShopperOne cart.
